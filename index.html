The protein-first pan formula: pick an anchor protein, add two sides, follow the timing card. Same three decisions every night. The flavors change; the thinking doesn't.

Protein numbers below are approximate ranges based on typical package labels. Brands vary, so check yours. Pick your own daily protein target; this is a cooking system, not a diet plan.

Card 1 — Chicken pan plate

Anchor: 6 oz (170 g) raw boneless chicken thigh or breast, cut into 1-inch pieces
Side A: 1 pouch microwave rice (about 1 cup cooked)
Side B: 2 cups frozen green beans or broccoli
Approx protein: 42–52 g per plate
Pan: one large skillet

Minute Do this
0 Heat skillet on medium-high with 1 tsp oil. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika.
2 Chicken in, single layer. Don't touch it for 4 minutes.
6 Flip and stir. Push chicken to one side, add frozen veg to the other with a splash of water. Lid on.
14 Lid off. Check chicken is cooked through (165°F / 74°C inside). Microwave the rice.
17 Plate. Squeeze of lemon or hot sauce on top.

Swaps: cheaper with thighs · faster with pre-cut chicken · dairy-free as written

Card 2 — Egg and turkey scramble plate

Anchor: 4 oz (113 g) 93% lean ground turkey + 3 large eggs
Side A: 1 whole-wheat tortilla or 2 slices toast
Side B: 1 cup baby spinach + salsa
Approx protein: 40–48 g per plate
Pan: one nonstick skillet

Minute Do this
0 Skillet on medium. Turkey in, break it up, season with salt, cumin, chili powder.
6 Turkey browned and cooked through. Add spinach, stir until wilted (1 minute).
7 Crack eggs into the pan, scramble everything together on low heat.
10 Warm the tortilla or toast while eggs set.
12 Plate with salsa.

Swaps: vegetarian with black beans instead of turkey (lower protein, about 30–35 g) · dairy-free as written · add 1/4 cup shredded cheese for +6–7 g

Card 3 — Forgot-to-thaw emergency plate

Nothing thawed. Nothing planned. This still lands.

Anchor: 1 can (5 oz) tuna or chicken in water, drained
Side A: 1 pouch microwave rice or quinoa
Side B: 1 bag pre-washed salad or 1 cup frozen edamame (microwaved)
Booster: 1/2 cup cottage cheese or 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt on the side
Approx protein: 40–55 g per plate (edamame and the booster add most of the range)
Pan: none, just a microwave and a bowl

Minute Do this
0 Microwave rice and edamame.
3 Drain the can. Mix with 1 tbsp mayo or Greek yogurt, mustard, salt, pepper.
5 Bowl: rice, salad or edamame, tuna mix on top. Booster on the side.
7 Eat.

Swaps: salmon pouch instead of tuna · dairy-free: skip the booster and double the edamame
